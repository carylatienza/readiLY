# ReadiLY

ReadiLY helps Grade 1–3 teachers give the government's oral reading tests (CRLA and Phil-IRI) to every child, offline, and fills in the DepEd form.

## Language

**Learner**:
A Grade 1–3 child whose oral reading is assessed. On the landing page, the learner's experience is the point of view the story is told from.
_Avoid_: Student (in the business plan "student" also means the founders), pupil (fine in teacher-facing prose, not as a term)

**Illustrative learner**:
A named, invented learner (Miguel, Bea, Paolo) used to tell the story. Always labelled as an example; never presented as a real child or quoted.
_Avoid_: Testimonial, case study, user

**School head**:
The principal who decides whether a school buys or pilots ReadiLY. The landing page's reader, together with Grade 1–3 teachers.
_Avoid_: Customer, admin

**Pilot list**:
The sign-up list of school heads and teachers interested in a pilot. Joining it is the landing page's one action.
_Avoid_: Waitlist, demo request

**Design target**:
A number ReadiLY is built to reach but has not yet measured in a classroom (e.g. about three minutes per child).
_Avoid_: Result, benchmark, accuracy

## Example dialogue

> **Teacher:** "Is Miguel a real pupil from your pilot?"
> **Founder:** "No, Miguel is an **illustrative learner**. We have no classroom results yet, so every number about him is a **design target**."
