How it works

Four steps.
About three minutes.

ReadiLY is not a new test. It is the one Miguel's teacher already gives.

  1. 01

    Pick a child

    Choose the child and a passage matched to their grade. Four matched sets rotate through the year.

  2. 02

    Read aloud

    The tablet records on the device. Shy readers like Bea get a one-minute warm-up read, recorded but never scored.

  3. 03

    See the result

    In about thirty seconds: words correct per minute, accuracy and the official level, with every mistake labelled.

  4. 04

    Check & export

    Tap any flagged word to change it. Then export the DepEd spreadsheet, filled in. You still upload it yourself.

Why it is stricter

A test shouldn't
flatter Miguel.

Illustrative example
What Miguel read

“They buy … bread and a bag of … rice.”

What tidy speech software writes

“They buy bread, eggs and a bag of rice.”

What ReadiLY writes

“They buy bread, eggs and a bag of rice.” 1 word skipped

Most speech software guesses what you meant. For a reading test that is the wrong thing to do: the score rises for exactly the children the test exists to find. ReadiLY writes down only what it hears and cannot invent a word the child did not say.

Strong reader
97%+

A word-for-word perfect reading must score 97% or higher, so a strong reader is never marked down.

Build target · Not a measured classroom result
Struggling reader, like Miguel
8 / 10

A passage with ten words left unread must have at least eight caught as omissions, so a weak reader is never rescued.

These are build targets, not results. Two trained human examiners scoring the same child disagree about one time in five. Matching a careful human is our bar.
